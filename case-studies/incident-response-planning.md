# Case Study 1 — Incident Response Planning

**Source:** SEC-320 Lab 1, Group 9 (academic group submission)  
**Type:** Simulated incident-response planning exercise

## Scenario
The report described multiple failed login attempts followed by a successful login from a blacklisted IP address, with a scenario involving potential unauthorized access and sensitive data exposure.

## Analysis documented in the group report
1. **Assessment:** Review login and remote-access logs to determine whether the access was authorized.
2. **Containment plan:** Isolate affected hosts, restrict compromised accounts, and block suspicious access.
3. **Eradication plan:** Revoke access, reset credentials, review active sessions, and strengthen controls.
4. **Recovery plan:** Audit systems and accounts, restore service after validation, and continue monitoring.
5. **Communications:** Notify stakeholders and document the incident.

The group also applied the **OODA loop** (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act) to its proposed response and mapped credential misuse to **MITRE ATT&CK T1078 — Valid Accounts**.

## What this demonstrates
Understanding of incident-response stages, evidence sources, prioritization, and communicating a proposed response.

## Limitations
This is a written classroom scenario. Although the source report uses phrases such as “blocked” and “isolated” in a summary table, it does **not provide system evidence that those actions were actually executed**. They are therefore presented here as *proposed* actions.
